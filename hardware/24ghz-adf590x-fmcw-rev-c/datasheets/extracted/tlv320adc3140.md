# TLV320ADC3140 — 4-Channel (up to 8 PDM) Burr-Brown Audio ADC

**Source PDF:** `datasheets/tlv320adc3140.pdf` (TI SBAS993B, May 2019 – rev. Oct 2019, 124 page-objects). Downloaded from ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv320adc3140.pdf (only host that responded reliably).
**Package:** 24-pin WQFN (RTW), 4.00 mm × 4.00 mm, 0.5 mm pitch, with exposed thermal pad. (p.3 Device Comparison; p.4 Fig; Package Addendum p.116)
**Role in Rev C:** Baseband ADC. Digitizes the four ADF5904 differential Rx baseband channels (via receiver amps). Control over I²C or SPI; audio out over TDM/I²S/LJ. Supports high sample rates for FMCW beat-frequency capture. (p.1, p.3)

## Supply rails and max currents
- **AVDD: 3.0/3.3/3.6 V (3.3 V mode) or 1.7/1.8/1.9 V (1.8 V mode, AREG internal reg shut down).** (Recommended Operating Conditions, p.6)
- **IOVDD: 3.0/3.3/3.6 V or 1.65/1.8/1.95 V.** (p.6)
- AREG to AVSS abs max 2.0 V (internal 1.8 V reg); DREG 1.5 V nominal digital core. IOVDD abs max 3.9 V. (Abs Max p.6; pins p.5)
- **Supply current (typ, 4-channel, PLL on, f_S = 48 kHz, BCLK=256×f_S):** I_AVDD = **21.3 mA** (internal AREG) / 20.2 mA (AVDD 1.8 V); I_IOVDD 0.1 mA. 2-channel @48kHz PLL off: I_AVDD 11.3 mA. Design-parameter spec: AVDD > 23 mA (4-ch, PLL on, 48 kHz). (Electrical Char. p.10; Table 131 p.107)
- Shutdown current < 1 µA (hardware, SHDNZ=0); sleep mode < 10 µA. (p.9, p.107)
- Abs max: AVDD to AVSS −0.3 to +3.9 V; AREG to AVSS −0.3 to +2.0 V; IOVDD to VSS −0.3 to +3.9 V; analog input to AVSS −0.3 to AVDD+0.3 V; T_A −40 to +125 °C; T_j 150 °C. ESD: HBM ±2000 V, CDM ±500 V. θ_JA 32.6 °C/W. (Abs Max & Thermal, p.6–7)

## Sample rate / interface capability
- **Programmable output data sample rate: 7.35 kHz to 768 kHz.** (ADC Other Parameters, p.9)
- **Supported FSYNC rates (auto-detected, PLL on): multiples/submultiples of 48 kHz — 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 96, 192, 384, 768 kHz; and of 44.1 kHz — 7.35 … 705.6 kHz.** BCLK/FSYNC ratios 16 to 2048 (subject to per-rate BCLK-frequency limits in Table 6/Table 7). (PLL & Clock Generation, p.25)
- Output word length programmable 16 to 32 bits. (p.9)
- Interfaces: **I²C or SPI** control; **TDM / I²S / left-justified (LJ)** audio out. GPIO1/GPIx can be MCLK input (max 36.864 MHz) if PLL bypassed. (p.3, p.25, p.6)
- Analog inputs AC- or DC-coupled; AC-coupled differential recommended for best DR. Full-scale differential input 2 V_RMS (AVDD 3.3 V). SNR 106 dB, DR 106 dB (0-dB gain, 10 kΩ). (p.7, p.26)

## Register map summary (Register Maps, p.59–)
- **8-bit registers, page-based.** 256 pages of 128 registers each. **Page 0 = device configuration** (default at power-up / after software reset). **Pages 2, 3, 4 = programmable biquad coefficients** (up to 12 biquads). PAGE[7:0] in register 0 of every page selects the current page. (p.59)
- Access procedure: write page N to register 0, then read/write valid registers in page N; repeat. Do not access reserved pages/registers. (p.59)
- Key page-0 registers (Table 50, p.59–60): P0_R0 PAGE_CFG; P0_R1 SW_RESET (bit0=1 → full POR reset, self-clearing); P0_R2 SLEEP_CFG (wake/sleep, AREG_SELECT); P0_R5 SHDN_CFG; P0_R7–R9 ASI_CFG0/1/2; P0_R11–R18 ASI_CH1–8 slot config; P0_R19/R20 MST_CFG0/1 (master mode); P0_R22 CLK_SRC; P0_R23/R24 (bias); P0_R59 BIAS_CFG; P0_R60–R99 CHx_CFG0–4 (channel config incl. CHx_INSRC input-source select, CHx_DC coupling); P0_R107/R108 DSP_CFG0/1 (incl. BIQUAD_CFG); P0_R112 AGC_CFG0; P0_R115 IN_CH_EN (input channel enable); P0_R116 ASI_OUT_CH_EN (output slot enable); P0_R117 PWR_CFG (power up ADC/MICBIAS/PLL); P0_R118/R119 DEV_STS0/1 (status). (p.59–60)
- Input source select (Table 8, p.26): CH1_INSRC = 00 analog differential (default), 01 analog single-ended, 10 digital PDM, 11 reserved. DC-coupling per channel via CHx_DC bits. Anti-alias/biquad filters in DSP path. (p.26, p.33)

## Power-up / startup sequence (Detailed Design Procedure p.107; Power Supply Recommendations p.113)
1. **Apply power** — power up IOVDD and AVDD with **SHDNZ held low** (device in hardware shutdown, <1 µA). IOVDD and AVDD may ramp in any order; supply ramp rate slower than 1 V/µs; t1/t2 ≥100 µs.
2. **Shutdown → sleep** — release SHDNZ high only after IOVDD/AVDD settle; wait ≥1 ms for internal register init (TI also states delay any I²C/SPI ≥2 ms after SHDNZ release / software reset). Device enters sleep (<10 µs... <10 µA).
3. **Sleep → active** — write P0_R2 to disable sleep; wait ≥1 ms for wake-up; (optionally override config/coefficients); enable input channels (P0_R115); enable ASI output slots (P0_R116); **power up ADC, MICBIAS, PLL (P0_R117)**; apply FSYNC + BCLK at the desired sample rate and BCLK/FSYNC ratio → recording data streams on the TDM bus.
4. **Active → sleep** — write P0_R2 to sleep; wait ≥6 ms (at 48 kHz) for volume ramp-down; read P0_R119; when P0_R119_D7=1 stop FSYNC/BCLK; sleep retains registers.
5–8. Repeat mode transitions; assert SHDNZ low for hardware shutdown; re-enter via step 2.
- Example line-input 4-channel I²C script (addr 0x98): `w 98 02 81` (wake, internal AREG), `w 98 73 F0` (enable IN CH1–4), `w 98 74 F0` (enable ASI out slots), `w 98 75 E0` (power up ADC/MICBIAS/PLL), then apply FSYNC/BCLK. (p.108)
- AREG: if AVDD < 1.98 V, short AREG to AVDD and keep AREG_SELECT=0 (default); if AVDD > 2.7 V, may set AREG_SELECT=1 to use internal regulator. (p.113)

## Application-circuit decoupling (Fig.168 p.110; Layout p.114)
- **AVDD: 10 µF + 0.1 µF + 1 µF** to GND (plus VREF 1 µF, AREG 0.1 µF nearby). **DREG: 10 µF + 0.1 µF.** **IOVDD: 10 µF + 0.1 µF.** VREF: 1 µF filter cap. MICBIAS: 1 µF (low ESR). AVSS/VSS thermal pad to ground plane. (Fig.168, p.110)
- I²C/SPI lines with series R and pull-ups per host. AC-couple differential Rx-baseband analog inputs (recommended for best DR).

## Spec deltas
- **384 kHz+ sample rate confirmed supported.** Programmable ODR to 768 kHz; FSYNC of 384 kHz and 768 kHz explicitly in the supported-rate table (48 kHz family). Matches spec assumption. For a 24.15–24.25 GHz FMCW chirp the ADC ODR sets the max resolvable beat frequency — 384 kHz is available; verify BCLK/FSYNC ratio per Table 6 keeps BCLK within limits at the chosen ODR. (p.9, p.25)
- **Exposed-pad exact size + TI recommended land pattern are missing from this datasheet copy** — the SBAS993B PDF ends at the Package Option Addendum and tape/reel data; the RTW0024 mechanical drawing is not embedded. **This is a gap for the pin-level/land verification gate.** Package body 4.00 × 4.00 mm and 0.5 mm pitch are confirmed (comparison table, tape A0/B0=4.25). The schematic/layout task must fetch the standalone TI "RTW0024" package drawing for EP dimensions and recommended land. Not a design contradiction, but a documentation gap flagged for the caller.
- ADC baseband is capacitively (AC) coupled and expects a differential input; this is consistent with the ADF5904 900 Ω differential baseband via receiver amps — no contradiction. (p.26)

- **Body size: 4.15/3.85 mm × 4.15/3.85 mm** (nominal 4.00 × 4.00 mm), **height: 0.8 mm max** (0.00–0.05 mm standoff). (p.50, "PACKAGE OUTLINE — RTW0024B")
- **Pin pitch: 0.5 mm** (24X 0.5 mm lead pitch; 20X 0.5 mm dimension callout). Lead width 0.3/0.18 mm (24X). (p.50)
- **Exposed thermal pad (EP): 2.45 mm ± 0.1 mm square** (pin/pad "25"). (p.50, EP dimension callout "☐2.45±0.1")
- **Recommended land pattern** (p.51, "EXAMPLE BOARD LAYOUT"):
  - 24 perimeter lead lands: **0.6 mm × 0.24 mm** pads (24X (0.6), 24X (0.24)) at **0.5 mm pitch** (20X (0.5)), rounded corners R0.05 mm typ.
  - Overall land-pattern footprint: **3.8 mm × 3.8 mm** (outer land envelope).
  - Thermal-pad land: **2.45 mm square** (☐2.45 reference, matches EP size).
  - Thermal-pad via layout: **4 vias, Ø0.2 mm typ**, arranged in a 2×2 grid under the pad at **0.97 mm × 0.97 mm** spacing from center (SYMM), i.e. vias offset ±0.485 mm from center in X and Y. Solder-mask detail: non-solder-mask-defined (NSMD) preferred for perimeter pads, 0.07 mm clearance all around; solder-mask-defined (SMD) alternative shown with 0.07 mm min metal-under-mask overlap. (p.51)
  - Guidance note: for more info see TI literature SLUA271 ("QFN and SON PCB Attachment"). (p.51, note 3)
- **Recommended stencil / solder-paste design** (p.52, "EXAMPLE STENCIL DESIGN"):
  - Based on **0.125 mm thick stencil**.
  - Thermal-pad paste aperture: **split into 4 quadrant openings, each 1.08 mm square** (4X ☐1.08), separated by **0.64 mm typ** gaps (cross-hair gap pattern) — avoids one large paste deposit under the pad.
  - Perimeter lead apertures: same as land pads, 24X (0.6) × 24X (0.24) at 0.5 mm pitch.
  - Target **78% printed solder paste coverage by area** under the package (exposed pad 25). (p.52)
  - Note: laser-cut apertures with trapezoidal walls/rounded corners may improve paste release; IPC-7525 may offer alternate recommendations. (p.52, note 4)
